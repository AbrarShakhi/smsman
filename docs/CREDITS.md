# Credits

SmsMan is built on open-source software and freely available design resources. We are grateful to their authors and contributors.

## Open-source libraries

### Android Jetpack

Developed by Google and the Android Open Source Project, and licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

- **Jetpack Compose:** the UI toolkit, including Foundation, Material 3 and Material Icons.
- **Navigation 3:** navigation and back stack management.
- **Lifecycle and Activity:** lifecycle-aware components and Compose integration.
- **Room:** local storage for favourites and pins.
- **DataStore:** storage for preferences.
- **Core and SplashScreen:** platform compatibility and the launch screen.
- **Graphics Shapes:** shape creation and morphing.

### Kotlin

Developed by JetBrains and licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

- **Kotlin standard library:** the core language library.
- **kotlinx.coroutines:** asynchronous and reactive programming.
- **kotlinx.serialization:** serialisation of navigation state.

### Koin

Dependency injection framework, developed by Arnaud Giuliani and Kotzilla, and licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). <https://insert-koin.io>

### Lottie

Animation library, developed by Airbnb and licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). <https://github.com/airbnb/lottie-android>

### MaterialKolor

Dynamic colour scheme generation, developed by Jordon de Hoog and licensed under the [MIT License](https://opensource.org/license/mit). <https://github.com/jordond/materialkolor>
